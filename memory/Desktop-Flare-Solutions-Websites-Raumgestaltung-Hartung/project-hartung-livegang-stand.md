Decision 2026-09-18: leave the report red, never restart the validation, documented in `src/vercel.ts` and `docs/LIVEGANG.md` section 9h.

Side effect found on 2026-09-18: the rule `/assets/(.*).mp4` covers ALL FOUR delivered video files, not just the decorative loop. Measured with ffprobe and curl: hero-schleife-4x5 and -9x16 each 47.2 s with no audio (decoration), `rundgang-gesamt` 70.2 s with AAC (the company film on the start page), `bad-rundgang` 48.0 s with AAC (on /badsanierung). Norman's two real films are therefore out of Google video search too, which nobody decided. Kept broad for now because both carry the app music with the unresolved licence (Befund 1 in `docs/audits/DSGVO-RECHT-2026-08-24.md`, open since 2026-08-31); narrowing the rule to `hero-schleife-*` belongs in the same work step as clearing that music. FINAL decision by Hannes on 2026-09-18, after he was shown that video-search visibility would require one dedicated watch page per film: **"Wir machen keine neue Seite"** - no new pages, the films shall NOT appear in video search. The broad `/assets/(.*).mp4` rule is therefore no longer an oversight but the implementation of that decision, and it stays broad on purpose. Narrowing it later needs a fresh client decision AND the music licence cleared (Befund 1, open since 2026-08-31). The music must stay audible; that needs no work because both films already carry `controls` and no `muted`, so the header only affects search engines, never visitors. Do not re-open this or propose film pages again unless Hannes asks.
